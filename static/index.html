Where the numbers come from

  • Usage made on this Mac only. Plan limits are account-wide, so they include your other devices.
  • Claude Code and Codex sessions are read from their logs; Antigravity from its conversation files. Calls that save no log (ai-trader's and price-watch's claude -p and codex exec) are counted through local telemetry from the time it was switched on.
  • The Claude limit is read with a one-word Haiku request every 20 minutes. ChatGPT limits come from the latest Codex session, so they are as fresh as your last Codex use. Antigravity does not expose its credit balance.
