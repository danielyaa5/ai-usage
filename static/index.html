Where the numbers come from

  • Usage made on this Mac only. Plan limits are account-wide, so they include your other devices.
  • Claude Code and Codex sessions are read from their logs; Antigravity from its conversation files. Calls that save no log (background jobs' claude -p --no-session-persistence and codex exec --ephemeral) are counted through local telemetry from the time it was switched on.
  • The Claude limit is read with a one-word Haiku request every 20 minutes. ChatGPT limits are asked of Codex every 10 minutes (no model usage). Antigravity limits are read every 5 minutes from the Antigravity app's local helper while the app is open on this Mac (no model usage).
